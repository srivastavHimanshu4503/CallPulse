# IndiAI — Where we are, where we're going

_A simple story of what we've built, what's next, and where this ends up._
_Last updated: 2026-07-27_

---

## The one-line story

**We're building the receptionist that every Indian business would hire if they could — one that speaks Hindi, English, and every mix in between, works 24 hours a day, never takes leave, and costs less than a phone recharge.**

---

## Why this needs to exist

Every small business in India has the same problem. The phone rings. Sometimes someone picks up. Most of the time, they don't. Every unanswered call is a customer who found a competitor.

A human receptionist costs ₹20,000–25,000 a month. They work 9 to 7. They speak two languages, on a good day. When they leave for lunch, the phone doesn't wait.

**IndiAI answers every call. In the caller's own language. Any time of the day or night. Books the appointment, captures the lead, sends it to the owner's WhatsApp — before the caller has even hung up.**

That's the whole product in one paragraph.

---

## What we've built so far

We spent the last several months getting the voice right. That was the hardest part, and it's now done.

**Today, an IndiAI agent can:**

- **Pick up any call, in any Indian language.** Hindi, English, Hinglish, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati. The agent switches naturally mid-sentence, the way real Indians talk.

- **Sound human.** Not robotic. Not "please press 1." A warm voice that laughs, apologises, asks follow-up questions.

- **Never miss a call.** 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Diwali night, Sunday morning, 3 AM — the agent picks up on the second ring.

- **Book appointments and capture leads.** The agent doesn't just talk. It writes down the customer's name, what they wanted, when they want to come in, their budget — and pushes it straight into the CRM.

- **Transfer to the owner when needed.** If the caller wants to talk to a human, the agent connects them in seconds. No hold music, no confusion.

- **Send recordings and transcripts to the owner.** Every call is recorded and summarised. The owner can read what happened in ten seconds instead of listening to 3 minutes.

- **Work on any Indian phone number.** We plug into both Indian and international phone providers, so a business anywhere in India can be up and running in ten minutes.

**Voice is stable. Voice is our foundation. Now we build on top of it.**

## What's coming next

We've split the future into three chapters. Each one adds a new dimension to what IndiAI can do.

### **Chapter 1 — Everywhere the customer is (next 3 months)**

Right now, IndiAI is a voice. Next, it becomes a presence.

- **WhatsApp** — Once our WhatsApp Business verification is approved (in progress), the same agent that answered the phone will pick up on WhatsApp too. Same persona, same memory of past calls, same intelligence.

- **Better voice, in every language.** We keep tuning the accent, the warmth, the pauses. The goal is that a first-time caller can't tell they're talking to an AI for the first thirty seconds.

- **Templates for the top 5 businesses that need us most.** Dental clinics, salons, real-estate brokers, coaching centers, restaurants. Each gets a version of IndiAI already trained for their world — knows the vocabulary, asks the right questions, follows up the right way.

- **Ten-minute setup.** A business owner should be able to sign up, connect their number, and get their first AI call — all before their tea gets cold.

**Chapter 1 is about polish and reach. Same product, more places, easier to start.**

### **Chapter 2 — The agent that grows the business (6 to 12 months)**

The agent stops being just a receptionist. It becomes a growth engine.

- **Outbound calling.** Not just answering — reaching out. Follow up with old leads. Wish customers on birthdays. Confirm appointments. Bring back people who almost bought but didn't.

- **Multiple agents for one business.** A sales agent for new customers, a support agent for existing ones, a booking agent for appointments. They hand off to each other, and to humans, without the caller ever hearing a click.

- **The owner's voice.** Business owners can (with their permission) clone their own voice. Callers hear the owner they know, not a generic AI.

- **Payments while talking.** The agent can send a UPI link mid-call. "Should I send you the ₹500 booking link on WhatsApp?" — done in three seconds.

- **Connected to the tools businesses already use.** Zoho, HubSpot, Practo, Zomato, Shopify. The AI's leads flow into wherever the business already lives.

**Chapter 2 is about doing more than answering — actively growing revenue for every business we serve.**

### **Chapter 3 — The invisible layer under every Indian business (12 to 24 months)**

This is the destination.

- **A marketplace of specialists.** A dental-savvy IndiAI trained by an actual dental chain. A real-estate agent tuned by a top broker. Business owners pick the agent that already understands their world, in one click.

- **Beyond India.** The same India-native tech works for the UAE (Hindi + Arabic + English), Southeast Asia, and the Indian diaspora anywhere. We become the voice AI for the Global South.

- **Enterprise-ready.** Big clinics, big chains, big brands trust us with their data. Formal certifications (ISO, SOC 2), dedicated infrastructure, private deployment options.

- **The Bharat data moat.** After a hundred million minutes of Indian conversations, we have the best voice AI for India that has ever existed. No global AI company can catch up without our data.

**Chapter 3 is about becoming infrastructure. Every phone number in India, when it rings for a small business, eventually rings an IndiAI agent.**

## Why this becomes a real company, not a science project

**Three things make us different from a big-tech voice AI:**

1. **Built for India, not translated.** Every design choice — the voices, the price, the setup, the language handling — starts from an Indian business, not a Silicon Valley whiteboard. Big-tech AI struggles with Hinglish. Ours was born in it.

2. **Made for the owner-operator.** Not for tech teams. Not for enterprise procurement. For the salon owner who's frustrated that she keeps losing weekend calls. Ten-minute setup, ₹2,000-a-month pricing, no engineer required.

3. **Full stack, one dashboard.** Voice, WhatsApp, CRM, analytics — one product, one price. Competitors sell one piece and tell you to integrate the rest yourself. Small businesses don't have the time.
