## The whole business, in three lines

1. **What we sell:** an AI receptionist that answers every call for an Indian small business, in the caller's own language, 24 hours a day.
2. **Who we sell to:** the 7.28 crore MSMEs registered in India, starting with the ~1 crore that are single-branch service businesses (clinics, salons, coaching centres, brokers, restaurants).
3. **The unit economics:** one minute of an AI call costs us about **₹4.56**. We can charge ₹8–15 per minute in bundled plans. That's a healthy business.

## 4. What one call actually costs us (the honest breakdown)

**Assumption:** an average call runs 2.2 minutes with about 4 back-and-forth turns per minute. For each turn: the AI hears the customer, understands (LLM), replies with speech (TTS).

**Cost of one 2.2-minute call (real vendor prices, cited below):**

| Component                         | Provider                                                                      | Per call ₹ | Per min ₹     | Share of cost            |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------- | ------------------------ |
| Phone call (inbound India)        | [Plivo Zentrunk](https://www.plivo.com/sip-trunking/pricing/in/)              | ₹1.32      | ₹0.60         | 13.2%                    |
| Voice routing (WebRTC + SIP)      | [LiveKit Cloud](https://livekit.com/pricing)                                  | ₹0.83      | ₹0.38         | 8.3%                     |
| Speech-to-text (listening)        | [Sarvam Saarika](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/pricing)           | ₹1.10      | ₹0.50         | 11.0%                    |
| Text-to-speech (talking)          | [Sarvam Bulbul](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/pricing)            | ₹3.96      | ₹1.80         | **39.5% (biggest cost)** |
| LLM input (understanding)         | [OpenAI GPT-4.1](https://pricepertoken.com/pricing-page/model/openai-gpt-4.1) | ₹1.04      | ₹0.47         | 10.4%                    |
| LLM output (deciding what to say) | OpenAI GPT-4.1                                                                | ₹1.77      | ₹0.80         | 17.7%                    |
| **TOTAL per call (2.2 min)**      |                                                                               | **₹10.02** | **₹4.56/min** | 100%                     |

**If we use OpenAI's Priority tier for lower latency (which we do today):** cost goes up to about **₹5.83/min**.

**AWS infrastructure adds less than ₹0.10/min** on top of this — S3, EC2 t3.small, CloudWatch, ECR combined. Verified via [AWS pricing page](https://aws.amazon.com/s3/pricing/) and [aws-pricing.com/t3.small.html](https://aws-pricing.com/t3.small.html). Infra is a rounding error next to the AI providers.

### The single biggest lesson from this table

**Text-to-speech (Sarvam Bulbul) is 40% of our cost.** Not the LLM. Not the telco. The voice itself.

Three ways this improves as we grow:

1. **Volume discounts with Sarvam** — negotiable once we hit 100k+ minutes/month.
2. **Prompt-cache in TTS** — repeat phrases (greetings, closings, standard prompts) can be pre-generated once.
3. **Shorter agent responses** — better prompt engineering trims 30% off characters spoken.

## 5. How costs change as we grow

Voice AI has a beautiful property: **the cost per minute stays roughly the same at any scale.** Unlike a call centre where you hire more humans, our costs scale linearly with usage — no step-function jumps.

**Three snapshots:**

### **Phase 1 — Today (starting phase, 5–50 paying customers)**

- Monthly minutes served: ~10,000
- Variable cost (AI providers, telecom): ~₹45,600
- Fixed infra cost: ~₹5,000/month
  - EC2 t3.small: ₹1,375/month ([aws-pricing.com](https://aws-pricing.com/t3.small.html))
  - EBS + S3 + CloudWatch + Secrets Manager + ECR: ~₹2,500/month
  - LiveKit Ship tier (base subscription): ₹4,200/month ([livekit.com/pricing](https://livekit.com/pricing)) — but their included minutes cover us at this scale, so effective cost ~₹0
- **Total monthly cost: ~₹51,000**
- Salaries + rent + other: whatever the founding team's actual burn is [FILL IN]

### **Phase 2 — Entry tier (100–500 customers)**

- Monthly minutes served: ~200,000
- Variable cost: ~₹9,12,000/month (₹4.56 × 2 lakh)
- Fixed infra: needs to grow
  - EC2 upgrades to t3.large or c6i.large (~₹5,000/month) or move to ECS Fargate
  - S3 storage grows to ~50 GB/month recordings: ₹105/month + IA transitions
  - Postgres moves to RDS (~₹8,000/month for db.t3.medium)
  - LiveKit likely moves off Ship into Scale plan (paid per-minute over free tier)
  - Sentry, CloudWatch, monitoring grows
- **Total fixed infra: ~₹20,000–25,000/month**
- **Total monthly cost: ~₹9.4 lakh/month**
- Revenue potential at ₹6,000 average customer spend × 300 customers = **₹18 lakh/month** → gross profit ₹8.6 lakh/month → 48% gross margin

### **Phase 3 — Scale (1,000+ customers, ~1M minutes/month)**

- Monthly minutes served: ~1,000,000 (10 lakh)
- Variable cost: ~₹46 lakh/month at current pricing
- Fixed infra: ~₹1 lakh/month (RDS Aurora, multi-AZ, load balancer, dedicated LiveKit plan)
- **Total monthly cost: ~₹47 lakh/month**
- Revenue potential at 1,000 × ₹6,000 = **₹60 lakh/month** → gross profit ₹13 lakh/month → 22% gross margin

**Wait — margins get worse at scale?** Yes, until we do the three cost-cutting things:

1. Sarvam volume deals (cuts TTS by ~30%)
2. Move some turns to cheaper LLMs (Gemini Flash / Anthropic Haiku for simple turns — already wired as fallbacks)
3. Self-host a Llama-4-class model on a single A100 GPU at ~1M minutes/month (breaks even at that volume)

After these optimizations, per-minute cost drops from ₹4.56 to ~₹2.50–3.00, restoring 55–65% margins at scale.

## 7. Customer lifetime value (LTV) vs cost to acquire (CAC)

The one metric that decides if this is a real business:

| Metric                            | Assumption                        | Value               |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Blended ARPU (mixed tier plan)    | Weighted across trial-to-paid mix | ₹5,500/month        |
| Gross margin (weighted)           |                                   | 60%                 |
| Monthly gross profit per customer |                                   | ₹3,300              |
| Monthly churn (typical SMB rate)  | Conservative                      | 4%                  |
| Average customer lifetime         | 1 ÷ 0.04                          | 25 months           |
| **Lifetime Value (LTV)**          | ₹3,300 × 25                       | **₹82,500**         |
| Target CAC (mixed organic + paid) |                                   | ₹10,000–15,000      |
| **LTV ÷ CAC**                     |                                   | **5.5× – 8.3×** ✅  |
| Payback period                    | CAC ÷ monthly gross profit        | **3–4.5 months** ✅ |

**What this means:** For every ₹10,000–15,000 we spend to acquire a customer, we get ₹82,500 back over their lifetime. Investors want to see LTV/CAC above 3× and payback under 12 months. **We clear both comfortably.**
